## Task

Table Booking and Online Delivery

## Objective

The objective of this task is to analyze the availability of table booking and online delivery services among restaurants.

The analysis focuses on:

- Determining the percentage of restaurants that offer table booking.
- Determining the percentage of restaurants that offer online delivery.
- Comparing the average ratings of restaurants with and without table booking.
- Analyzing online delivery availability across different price ranges.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Table Booking and Online Delivery

The percentage of restaurants offering table booking and online delivery was calculated using the restaurant service information.

### 2. Average Rating Comparison

The average `Aggregate rating` was calculated separately for:

- Restaurants with table booking.
- Restaurants without table booking.

A bar chart was created to compare the two groups.

### 3. Online Delivery by Price Range

The availability of online delivery was analyzed across different `Price range` categories.

A percentage-based comparison was visualized using a bar chart.
