## Dataset
Restaurant Dataset

## Objective
Explore and preprocess the restaurant dataset to understand its structure, data quality, and important patterns.

## Work Performed

- Loaded the restaurant dataset using Pandas.
- Examined the first few records using `head()`.
- Checked the number of rows and columns.
- Reviewed the column names and dataset structure.
- Checked for missing values.
- Reviewed the data types of the columns.
- Analyzed the distribution of the `Aggregate rating` variable.
- Identified the most common rating category.
- Examined class imbalance in the rating distribution.

## Key Findings

- The dataset contains **9,551 rows and 21 columns**.
- The `Aggregate rating` variable was analyzed to understand the distribution of restaurant ratings.
- The rating **0.0** was the most common rating category, with **2,148 restaurants (22.49%)**.
- The rating distribution is imbalanced, with the 0.0 category occurring noticeably more frequently than several other rating categories.
